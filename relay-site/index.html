Questions, answered

What happens when it doesn’t know the answer?

It says so instead of guessing, offers a human, and asks for the customer’s email or phone. The conversation is flagged in your inbox.

Does it work in other languages?

Yes. It replies in the language your customer writes in, and the assistant for you can be set to your preferred language.

Where does my information go?

Your documents and settings are stored for your account only and used to answer questions for your business. Each reply is written by Claude, an AI model made by Anthropic.

    Billing, explained
    
      
    What happens on day 14?
    Your card is charged the plan price, then every month. We email you 3 days before, so there are no surprises.
    
      
    Why is there a $1 card check?
    It confirms your card works and lets us save it for billing after the trial. It’s refunded straight away, though your bank can take several business days to show it.
    
      
    How do I cancel?
    Open your Profile and choose Cancel plan. Cancel before day 14 and you pay nothing. After that you keep access until the end of the period you’ve paid for.
    
      
    Which currency do you charge in?
    US dollars. Your bank may add currency-conversion fees if your card is in another currency.
